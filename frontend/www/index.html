    SHARE YOUR LOCATION?

    FAST PATH CAN FIND THE NEAREST STOP OR THE NEXT BUS NEAR YOU AUTOMATICALLY IF YOU SHARE YOUR LOCATION. YOU CAN ALWAYS SKIP THIS AND JUST TYPE A STOP OR PLACE NAME INSTEAD.

    HOW TO USE FAST PATH

    START TYPING AND FAST PATH SUGGESTS STOPS, ROUTES, AND PLACES (WITH THE NEXT BUS TIME) -- TAP ONE FOR THE ANSWER. OR TYPE A FULL QUESTION AND PRESS ENTER. TRY THINGS LIKE:

    > WHEN IS THE NEXT BUS AT <STOP>? (OR JUST "WHEN'S
      THE NEXT BUS?" WITH LOCATION ON)
    > NEAREST STOP TO <PLACE>? (OR "...TO ME?")
    > WHERE IS <STOP, BUSINESS, OR STREET>?
    > NEAREST PHARMACY/GAS STATION/ETC?
    > FIRST/LAST BUS AT <STOP>?
    > LIST STOPS ON ROUTE <N>
    > TIMETABLE FOR ROUTE <N>?
    > FROM <PLACE> TO <PLACE>? (PLANS YOUR WHOLE TRIP)
    > PLAN <CITY> TO <CITY> (LONG-DISTANCE, NEEDS SIGNAL)
    > FARES (OR "FARES HART") -- PRICES & HOW TO PAY
    > WALKING DIRECTIONS TO <PLACE>?
      (FIRST TYPE "DOWNLOAD WALKING DIRECTIONS" ONCE -- A
      ONE-TIME ~164MB DOWNLOAD -- THEN THIS WORKS OFFLINE TOO)

    TAP [ LIVE MAP ] ABOVE TO SEE ROUTES AND BUSES IN REAL TIME. TAP A STOP FOR LIVE ARRIVALS, OR A ROUTE LINE TO FOLLOW IT FOR SERVICE ALERTS. ONCE SYNCED, EVERYTHING BUT LIVE BUS POSITIONS (AND LONG-DISTANCE TRIP PLANNING) WORKS OFFLINE.
