SHARE YOUR LOCATION?

THEBUS CAN FIND THE NEAREST STOP OR THE NEXT BUS NEAR YOU AUTOMATICALLY IF YOU SHARE YOUR LOCATION. YOU CAN ALWAYS SKIP THIS AND JUST TYPE A STOP OR PLACE NAME INSTEAD.

HOW TO USE THEBUS

TYPE A QUESTION AND PRESS ENTER. TRY THINGS LIKE:

> WHEN IS THE NEXT BUS AT <STOP>?
> NEAREST STOP TO <PLACE>? (OR "...TO ME?")
> FIRST/LAST BUS AT <STOP>?
> LIST STOPS ON ROUTE <N>

TAP [ LIVE MAP ] ABOVE TO SEE ROUTES AND BUSES IN REAL TIME. ONCE SYNCED, EVERYTHING BUT LIVE BUS POSITIONS WORKS OFFLINE.
