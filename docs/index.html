What is Fast Path?

A transit assistant that works even when your signal doesn't — in English or Spanish.

Fast Path is a free, offline-first transit assistant styled after a retro MS-DOS terminal. It merges GTFS schedule data from multiple Florida transit agencies into a single lightweight dataset that lives entirely on your phone — so once it's synced, checking a schedule, finding the nearest stop, or planning a trip works with zero signal. Ask it plain-language questions like "next bus at Avalon Publix" or "from Spring Hill to downtown Tampa" — or the same thing in Spanish, "próximo autobús en Avalon Publix" — and it answers instantly from data already on your device, in whichever language you're using. When you're online, Fast Path adds a live layer on top: real buses moving on the map across all three counties, live arrival predictions at your stop, how full the next bus is, arrival reminders that buzz before your ride, service alerts for detours and cancellations, push notifications when something affects a route you follow, and long-distance trip planning that can reach far beyond the tri-county area. There's still no account, no tracking, and nothing selling your data — just a fast, private way to catch the bus.

What It Does

Built to respect your data, your battery, and your commute.

💬

Ask in Plain Words

"Next bus at Avalon Publix," "first and last bus," "timetable for a route," "where is Spring Hill Dr" — type it how you'd say it, and Fast Path answers as you type.

🌐

English & Español

Flip the whole app to Spanish — and ask your questions in Spanish too. "Próximo autobús," "parada más cercana," "desde X hasta Y" all work, with answers in Spanish.

🗺️

Cross-Agency & Long-Distance Trips

Ask "from A to B" and Fast Path routes you even across agencies — Hernando to Tampa on HART, transfers and all — and can plan trips far beyond the tri-county area.

🛰️

Live Bus Map

Watch real buses move across all three counties — Hernando, Pasco, and HART — with their route lines drawn right on the map, over real street tiles.

🚌

See How Full the Bus Is

Live crowding, straight from the agency feed — tap a bus and see whether it's got room, is filling up, or is packed before you head to the stop.

⏱️

Real-Time Arrivals

Live "next arrival" predictions at your stop, straight from the agency's real-time feed — not just the printed schedule.

🔔

Arrival Reminders

Set a reminder on a stop and Fast Path buzzes you before the bus is due — so you can leave at the right minute instead of waiting at the curb.

⚠️

Service Alerts & Push

Detours, cancellations, and closures surface in the app — and follow the routes you ride to get a push notification the moment an alert hits one, even when the app is closed.

⭐

Favorite Stops Board

Star the stops you use most and get a one-word "favorites" board with the next few arrivals at each — your regular stops, always a tap away.

🧭

Offline Maps & Walking Directions

Download the whole regional basemap once, then pan the live map and get walking directions to your stop with no signal at all.

🎙️

Voice & Accessibility

Speak your question instead of typing, bump up the text size, and switch on high-contrast mode — built in, no setup.

📍

Find Stops, Places & Fares

Nearest stop to you or any landmark, "nearest pharmacy / gas / grocery" from a built-in OpenStreetMap corpus, plus each agency's fares and where to buy — all offline.

🔒

Private by Design

No accounts, no analytics, no location history stored anywhere. Your trips are yours.
