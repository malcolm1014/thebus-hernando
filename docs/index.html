What is TriBus?

A transit assistant that works even when your signal doesn't.

TriBus is a free, offline-first transit assistant styled after a retro MS-DOS terminal. It merges GTFS schedule data from multiple Florida transit agencies into a single lightweight dataset that lives entirely on your phone — so once it's synced, checking a schedule, finding the nearest stop, or planning a trip works with zero signal and zero backend calls. Ask it plain-English questions like "next bus to Brooksville" or "how do I get from Spring Hill to downtown Tampa," and it answers instantly from data already on your device. A second tab adds a live map with real-time bus positions whenever you do have a connection. There's no account, no tracking, and nothing phoning home in the background — just a fast, private way to catch the bus.
